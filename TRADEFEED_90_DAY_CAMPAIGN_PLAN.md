# TradeFeed — 90-Day Social Campaign Plan

**Campaign umbrella:** *The upgrade from chaos to a real shop.*  
**Primary line:** *One photo. One link. Orders on WhatsApp.*  
**Supporting closer:** *Your Shop. One Link. Sold on WhatsApp.*  
**Audience:** South African WhatsApp, Facebook Marketplace, and informal sellers who want to look and operate like a real shop without changing how their buyers order.

This plan schedules the production-ready concepts in `TradeFeed_Campaign_Brief.md`. That brief remains the source for the long-form production specs (prompts, camera, motion, audio, captions, and CTAs). This document supplies the release order, testing logic, and daily publishing assignments.

## Guardrails

- Keep the promise concrete: a photo becomes a listing; one link holds the catalogue; buyers order through WhatsApp.
- Use `#064e3b`, `#FAF8F4`, warm stone, and `#25D366` only for an action that opens WhatsApp. Avoid blue and loud gradients.
- Use real South African light, texture, neighbourhoods, products, and seller energy. Do not use generic "startup office" images or performative hustle language.
- Never put live counts, seller names, product totals, prices, plan limits, or verification statistics into a pre-rendered asset without checking the live site on the publishing day.
- Make UI claims only from the approved site experience. The verified product claims at campaign planning are: 20 products free, 10 AI listings/month, no card, setup under three minutes, AI listing generation in about 10 seconds, catalogue links, structured WhatsApp orders, seller verification, and order/reply-time visibility.
- Every paid unit needs a clean in-platform destination URL with `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`; never embed a long URL in artwork.

## Objectives and measurement

| Phase | Days | Job | Main metric | Decision rule |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Discover | 1–21 | Make the problem of chat-chaos unmistakable | 3-second video hold, completed view, landing-page view | Keep the two hooks with the best completed-view rate and remake them for every priority vertical. |
| Believe | 22–49 | Prove ease, trust, and product value | catalogue-link starts, sign-up conversion | Put paid weight behind the story that earns the most completed sign-ups, not merely cheap clicks. |
| Join | 50–70 | Build community and seller identity | activated seller (first product + shared link) | Retarget viewers and site visitors with setup and social-proof creative. |
| Convert & compound | 71–90 | Motivate sharing, referrals, and upgrades | active seller, referral signup, free-to-paid conversion | Recut the winning three creatives with one focused CTA per audience. |

**Suggested split:** 40% UGC/documentary, 30% problem-to-solution, 20% polished brand/product, 10% community or trend-responsive. Publish each core post natively to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts where appropriate; tailor caption length, cover, and sound to the platform.

## Production and paid-media checklist

1. Make the first nine posts before launch: three chaos, three setup, three trust. This creates enough alternatives for an honest first-week test.
2. Keep a 9:16 master without baked-in captions. Export a platform-native version with a safe text zone, a 1:1 Facebook feed crop, and a 4:5 Instagram feed crop.
3. Replace every synthetic product UI in a conversion ad with a current screen capture before spending money. AI video may establish emotion; it must not misrepresent the flow.
4. Use seller testimonials, faces, shop names, and buyer reactions only with written consent. Do not imply affiliation or invent outcomes.
5. Review each week: hook hold, completed view, click-to-sign-up, sign-up-to-first-product, first-product-to-shared-link. A beautiful ad that fails activation does not scale.
6. Refresh the winning hook monthly, not the brand system: new seller category, new first frame, same product truth and same TradeFeed visual language.

## First pilot: the creative to validate now

**Name:** *The Upgrade*  
**Objective:** earn an immediate pause from sellers whose business lives in WhatsApp, then make the promise feel calm and achievable.  
**Audience:** clothing, sneaker, beauty, and resale sellers; Facebook Marketplace and WhatsApp-business interests; Johannesburg, Durban, Queenstown, Soweto and expansion cities.  
**Psychology:** recognisable frustration → visual relief → low-risk next step.  
**Asset:** vertical poster / Reel cover. A genuine South African seller in a small, warmly lit clothing shop holds a phone displaying a clean emerald-and-cream product grid; soft real fabric textures, deliberate negative space for exact copy added in design software. No fictional dashboard stats, logos, or UI labels in the generated image.  
**Copy lock-up:** `Your shop deserves more than a chat.` / `One photo. One link. Orders on WhatsApp.` / `Start free today.`  
**Motion extension:** start tight on rushed message bubbles, then dissolve through a fabric-colour match cut into the clean catalogue; end on the seller’s relaxed look and a one-second CTA card.  
**Music:** restrained Amapiano pulse moving into a warm, optimistic synth/kalimba resolve.  
**Caption:** “Your customers are already on WhatsApp. Give them one place to see what you sell. Create your catalogue link free.”  
**Hashtags:** `#TradeFeedSA #WhatsAppBusiness #SmallBusinessSA #SellOnlineSA #SouthAfricanEntrepreneur`  
**CTA:** `Get your catalogue link`.
