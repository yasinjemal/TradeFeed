## 0. Brand Foundation (pulled live from tradefeed.co.za)

**Primary line:** "One photo. One link. Orders on WhatsApp."
**Campaign line (alt/closer):** "Your Shop. One Link. Sold on WhatsApp."
**Sub-line:** Take a photo — AI writes the listing in 10 seconds. Share your catalogue link and take orders where your customers already are. No app for them, no website for you.

**Real proof points (use verbatim — never invent numbers):**
- 78 live sellers · 215 products listed · 62 orders placed · 12 SA cities
- 20 products free forever · 10 AI listings/month on Free plan
- Set up in under 3 minutes · no credit card required
- Real sellers: MEN'S CORNERS (Johannesburg), smiley fashion shop (Queenstown), Omyfashions (Johannesburg), NLM Essentials Hub (Johannesburg), JONY HAFIZ (Durban)
- Pricing: Free (R0) → Starter (R99/mo) → Pro (R299/mo, most popular) → Pro AI (R499/mo)
- Order tracking format: TF-20260224-0042
- Payments via PayFast · POPIA compliant

**Color system:**
- Deep Emerald `#064e3b` (brand primary, matches live site `#059669` accent)
- Cream `#FAF7F0` / Warm Beige `#E8DCC8`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- WhatsApp Green `#25D366` (CTA/action accent only — never as dominant brand color)

**Typography direction:** Geometric sans (Inter/General Sans-style), tight tracking on headlines, generous line-height on body. Numerals should look editorial (Stripe/Linear influence).

**Visual references:** Apple product launch films, Stripe/Linear marketing sites, Airbnb warmth, Arc Browser's playful precision, Nike's emotional storytelling — filtered through African light, fabric, skin tone, and street texture. Never generic "AI stock" gloss: real grain, real imperfection, real warmth.

**Voice:** Plain, confident, respectful. Short sentences. No hustle-bro hype, no exclamation-point spam. TradeFeed talks to sellers like peers who've earned respect — because their businesses are real.

**Master negative prompt (apply to every AI generation):** distorted hands, extra fingers, warped faces, uncanny valley skin, plastic-looking skin, text artifacts, garbled typography, watermarks, stock-photo lighting, generic office backgrounds, oversaturated HDR, AI sheen, mismatched shadows, low resolution, blurry, deformed limbs, asymmetrical eyes.

**Universal CTA bank:** "Start free today." · "Get your catalogue link — it's free." · "Stop selling through chaos." · "Your shop, live in 3 minutes." · "78 sellers already did it."
